A strong research proposal starts with a clear scientific question, then connects that question to a feasible study design, measurable outcomes, and an analysis plan.

For biostatistics work, I try to make the assumptions visible early: population, exposure, outcome, confounding structure, missing data, and the statistical model that will support the final interpretation.
